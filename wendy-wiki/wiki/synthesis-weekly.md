## Purpose
Once a week, look across trading, health, and growth for patterns that
connect them. The kind of links a single-domain page would miss:

- Low sleep (health) → anxious mood → bad trade override (trading)
- Avoiding a big professional move (growth) → low energy (health)
- Playing small at work (growth) → distracted trading (trading)

## How to run it
In Claude Code, say:

> Run the weekly cross-domain synthesis.

Claude will:
1. Read the last 7 days of entries from `raw/trading/`, `raw/health/`,
   and `raw/growth/`
2. Read the current state of every page in `wiki/trading/`,
   `wiki/health/`, and `wiki/growth/`
3. Find concrete cause-and-effect chains that cross domains
4. Append a new dated section below — never overwrite prior weeks
5. Update the "Current understanding" block with the strongest 1-3
   cross-domain patterns Wendy is currently living inside

## Tone
Direct and blunt. Name the chain. Quote Wendy's own words. If a week
doesn't have enough data, say so and stop — do not invent links.
